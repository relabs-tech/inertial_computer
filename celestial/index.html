1 · Time by your watch (UTC) & position

Approximate is fine (DR): the position and time only fix the azimuths and the altitudes used for the corrections. The result does not depend on the watch being right.

2 · Bodies

Near limb = the Moon's edge facing the body; far limb = the edge away from it. For the Sun, use its matching limb (near–near or far–far).

3 · Sextant readings

Apparent altitude = sextant altitude of the body's centre corrected for index error and dip (not yet for refraction or parallax). Only about ±1′ is needed; the distance itself must be as sharp as you can measure.
